# 脚手架:民谣(慵懒/怀旧)

> 起点,不是模具。风格框是参考起点,骨架处处留白——大胆改、大胆删、填你自己的私货。

## 风格框(起点,描述式写法,见 reference/style-field.md)
```
A laid-back mid-tempo indie folk song in one intimate, dry room. Fingerstyle acoustic guitar leaves space around a warm mid-range male singer with clear pronunciation, natural breath breaks, and simple phrase endings. Brushed drums hold a steady pocket while bass sits slightly behind; a mournful harmonica answers only in vocal gaps and develops the main hook in the short solo. Sparse verses open gradually into a fuller chorus without changing tempo or register. No EDM or heavy autotune.
```
> 想模仿某嗓音/某人:别写名字,翻译成声学特征用描述句补进风格框。
> **点睛乐器**:默认口琴(harmonica),可换口风琴(melodica)/大提琴(cello)/颤音琴(vibraphone)——按情绪选一件,见 `reference/arrangement-dynamics.md`。

## 动态走向
默认**渐进升华型**(▁▂▃▄▅▆▇):主歌极简(吉他+人声),逐段加乐器,终副歌全进+口琴复现。
也可选**一路淡然型**:全程克制,越到后面越少,最后只剩人声+吉他——风格框改成 `quiet and understated throughout, never building to a loud climax`。

## 留白骨架
```
[Intro - harmonica melody, 4 bars]

[Verse 1 - sparse, just acoustic guitar and soft vocal]
〔一个具体画面/细节,别直说情绪;4-8行,行内字数错落,密集短句〕

[Pre-Chorus - building, brushed drums enter]
〔起势,2-4行,字数渐少〕

[Chorus - full band, layered harmonies, wide open]
〔围绕一个支点意象,平实但别致;舒展拉长,末行+语气词〕

[Verse 2 - sparse again, just guitar and vocal]
〔与 V1 段间对仗:第N行字数≈V1第N行〕

[Pre-Chorus - building]

[Chorus - full band, harmonies]

[Bridge - everything drops back, just vocal and harmonica]
〔转折/留白,最安静处;可换人称/换句式/缩到极简短句〕

[Harmonica Solo]

[Final Chorus - same singer and tempo, fuller drums, harmonica reprises the hook, light harmony]
〔第三次保持主旋律,只增加少量配器与和声;或反高潮缩简〕
(语气词拉升)

[Outro - just vocal and harmonica, fading into silence]
```

## 调参
- 跑调:换 Key ±2-3 半音多生成几版。
- 更慵懒:在描述里加 `The vocal is lazy and breathy, with a hazy atmosphere.`
- 更怀旧:在描述里加 `Warm analog tape sound with vinyl warmth, evoking 90s Chinese indie folk.`
- **要反高潮**:终副歌标签改 `[Final Chorus - stripped back to just vocal and guitar, quiet]`,Outro 更留白。
- **换点睛乐器**:风格框里 harmonica 换成 `a mournful cello line beneath the vocal` 或 `a soft melodica melody floating above`。
