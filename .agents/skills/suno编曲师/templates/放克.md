# 脚手架:放克(70s funk / neo-funk)

> 起点,不是模具。funk 的灵魂是 groove——贝斯线和切分节奏驱动一切,管乐点缀。骨架留白,自由发挥。

## 风格框(起点,描述式写法)
```
A groovy 70s funk track at about 100 BPM in one warm studio room. Live drums hold a steady syncopated pocket; slap bass locks to the kick while wah guitar and clavinet trade short rhythmic spaces. The horn section answers only at phrase endings instead of playing continuously. One confident mid-range male singer keeps the same gritty, soulful timbre and natural breath pattern throughout. Preserve dynamics and light room bleed. No EDM or robotic vocal processing.
```
> 想要女声:界面按钮切女声,描述改成 `confident female vocal with a sassy, powerful delivery`。
> **点睛乐器**:默认击弦古钢琴(clavinet,funk 的秘密武器),可换班卓琴(banjo)/滑棒吉他(lap steel)——越反差越有趣。

## 动态走向
默认**高开平走型**(█▇▆▆▅▆▅):一上来 groove 就满,靠持续张力而非渐强。Bridge 收一下做反差。

## 留白骨架
```
[Intro - bass line + horn stabs, 4 bars]
〔管乐 intro 或贝斯 line 起手〕

[Verse 1 - groove established, vocal rhythmic]
〔短句,有节奏感,贴 groove;行内错落;密集〕

[Pre-Chorus - horns build, drums fill]
〔起势,鼓加花;字数渐展〕

[Chorus - horn answers in vocal gaps, short group response, same pocket]
〔一句能跟唱的 hook,有劲;舒展;+ 管乐 stabs / 语气词 (hey!)〕

[Verse 2 - groove continues, clavinet more prominent]
〔与 V1 段间对仗〕

[Pre-Chorus]

[Chorus]

[Bridge - groove drops back, just bass and clavinet]
〔收一下,贝斯 solo 或最省的留白;换打法〕

[Bass Solo]   （或 [Horn Solo] / [Clavinet Solo]）

[Final Chorus - same pocket, fuller horn responses, restrained gang vocals]
〔保持同一 groove,只增加管乐回应和少量群唱〕
(hey! get on the groove!)

[Outro - bass line + horn stabs, fade or cold stop]
```

## 调参
- 更复古:在描述里加 `warm analog tape, vinyl crackle, vintage 70s production`。
- 更现代:改成 `tight modern neo-funk with clean production, crisp drums, subtle synth accents`。
- 更摇滚:加 `distorted riffing guitar, heavier drums, funk rock energy`。
- groove 不够紧:怪异参考度降到 40,多生成几版挑 pocket 最稳的。
- **换点睛乐器**:clavinet 换成 `a driving banjo rolling underneath` 或 `a lap steel gliding over the groove`。
