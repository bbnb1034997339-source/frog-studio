# 脚手架:爵士(vocal jazz / cool jazz / fusion)

> 起点,不是模具。jazz 的灵魂是 swing feel + 即兴交织 + 呼吸感。人声爵士要"贴着麦唱"的亲密感,器乐爵士靠乐器对话。骨架留白,自由发挥。

## 风格框(起点,描述式写法)
```
A smoky late-night vocal jazz track in one intimate dry room. Brushed drums keep a light, steady swing while upright bass sits just behind; Rhodes piano, tenor sax, and vibraphone answer one another only between vocal phrases. One low, close-mic female singer uses natural breath breaks, simple endings, and very limited melodic runs. Keep the same tempo, register, and room throughout, with a short solo that varies the main hook. No EDM pulse or heavy autotune.
```
> 想要器乐爵士(纯音乐):开"纯音乐"开关,描述改成 `A relaxed cool jazz piece — muted trumpet and breathy tenor sax trading melodic lines over walking bass and brushed drums. Warm, intimate, slightly improvisational feel.`
> 想要男声:界面按钮切男声,描述改成 `male vocal, warm baritone, crooning and intimate`。
> **点睛乐器**:默认颤音琴(vibraphone,爵士标准配色),可换大提琴(cello)/竖琴(harp)。

## 动态走向
默认**渐进升华型**:主歌极亲密(钢琴+人声)→ 副歌加贝斯鼓和 sax → 终副歌 sax 即兴交织最丰满。

## 留白骨架
```
[Intro - Rhodes piano + vibraphone, slow and intimate]

[Verse 1 - just piano and vocal, close-mic]
〔贴麦的亲密短句,有呼吸感;行内错落;密集低语〕

[Pre-Chorus - upright bass and brushed drums ease in]
〔起势,字数渐展〕

[Chorus - full trio, sax weaves between vocal lines, vibraphone glimmers]
〔一个支点意象,平实别致;舒展;+ 萨克斯回应 / 语气词 (mmm)〕

[Verse 2 - fuller than V1 but still intimate]
〔与 V1 段间对仗〕

[Pre-Chorus]

[Chorus]

[Bridge - everything drops to just vocal and vibraphone]
〔最留白;换打法——可换成排比长句或极简短句〕

[Vibraphone Solo]   （或 [Sax Solo] / [Piano Solo] / [Trumpet Solo]）
〔即兴感,乐器对话〕

[Final Chorus - fullest, sax improvising on top, harmonies swell]
〔末段+即兴装饰推高潮〕
(mmm... 就是这样)

[Outro - just vocal and vibraphone, then silence]
```

## 调参
- 更 smoky/深夜:在描述里加 `late-night, smoky room, close and dry, intimate`。
- 更 fusion/ energetic:改成 `An energetic jazz fusion track — electric piano, fretless bass, syncopated drums, clean electric guitar lines. Dynamic and virtuosic but organic, live ensemble feel, upbeat.`
- swing 不够:怪异参考度提到 55,段落标签强调 `swing feel, brushed drums, walking bass`。
- 想加国风:在描述里织入 `subtle Chinese elements — bamboo flute accents, pentatonic melody hints`。
- **换点睛乐器**:vibraphone 换成 `a mournful cello line beneath` 或 `delicate harp arpeggios`。
