# 脚手架:韩流(K-pop ballad / K-R&B / K-pop band)

> 起点,不是模具。K-pop 范围很广——抒情 ballad、K-R&B、乐队风。本项目偏好有人味、不舞曲化的方向。骨架留白,自由发挥。

## 风格框(起点,描述式写法)

### 抒情 ballad
```
A polished Korean pop ballad with one clear, expressive mid-range male singer — emotional and controlled, with natural breath breaks and the same vocal timbre throughout. Sparse piano gradually opens into strings and restrained backing harmonies, while a delicate harp quietly previews and reprises the main hook. Keep one warm studio space and preserved dynamics at a steady mid-tempo. No EDM, no trap elements, no heavy autotune.
```

### K-R&B(慵懒)
```
A smooth K-R&B track at about 85 BPM in one late-night studio space. Crisp live drums hold a steady syncopated groove while warm bass sits slightly behind; Rhodes piano leaves gaps for a vibraphone to preview and answer the main hook. The same breathy mid-range male singer uses natural breath breaks and limited melodic runs throughout. Restrained harmonies join only on key chorus words. No EDM/trap pulse or heavy autotune.
```

### K-pop band(乐队风,明亮)
```
A warm K-pop band track performed in one bright studio room. Jangly guitars leave gaps around a clear mid-range female singer, while live drums and bass hold the same upbeat pocket from beginning to end. A glockenspiel previews the hook and returns between phrases; a small group response joins only on the chorus hook. Keep the same singer, register, tempo, and room, with preserved dynamics. No EDM/trap pulse or heavy autotune.
```
> K-pop 容易漂向舞曲化,风格框末尾的否定句务必带。中韩英混唱:歌词直接写混合语言,不要在风格框说明。人声性别用界面按钮设。
> **点睛乐器**:ballad 默认竖琴(harp),K-R&B 默认颤音琴(vibraphone),band 默认钢片琴(glockenspiel)——按情绪换。

## 动态走向
- **Ballad**:渐进升华型——主歌极简(钢琴+人声)→ 副歌加弦乐铺开 → 终副歌增加和声与配器,主唱仍保持原声线和舒适音域。
- **K-R&B**:渐进升华型(温和)——靠质感和和声堆叠,不炸。
- **K-pop band**:高开平走型——一上来就亮,靠持续活力。

## 留白骨架
```
[Intro - piano/guitar/synth pad, slow build, signature instrument enters]
〔钢琴/吉他/合成器铺底,慢起,点睛乐器浮现〕

[Verse 1 - sparse, just piano/guitar and vocal]
〔短句叙事,贴氛围;行内错落;可中韩英混;密集〕

[Pre-Chorus - building, strings/groove enters]
〔起势,情绪拉升;字数渐展〕

[Chorus - full arrangement, layered harmonies, wide open]
〔一句能跟唱的 hook,有情感冲击;舒展;+ 和声 / 语气词 (oh / yeah)〕

[Verse 2 - fuller than V1]
〔与 V1 段间对仗〕

[Pre-Chorus]

[Chorus]

[Bridge - everything drops to just vocal and piano/signature instrument]
〔转折/留白,最安静处;换打法——换人称或缩到极简〕

[Guitar Solo]   （或 [Piano Solo] / [Strings Interlude]）

[Final Chorus - same singer, key and tempo; strings and harmonies grow fuller]
〔末段靠弦乐/和声/力度推高潮,保持原声线与合理音域;或反高潮缩简〕
(oh... 영원히 / forever)

[Outro - just vocal and signature instrument, fading]
〔渐弱,人声收尾〕
```

## 调参
- 更抒情:在描述里加 `building from sparse piano to a fuller emotional chorus through layered strings and restrained harmony, while the singer remains in the same comfortable register`。
- 更 K-R&B/律动:加 `syncopated groove, finger snaps, warm analog, late-night feel`。
- 防舞曲化:确认风格框末尾有 `no EDM, no four-on-the-floor beat, no trap`,怪异参考度≤50。
- 想要组合感(多人声):在描述里织入 `layered harmonies, gang vocals on the chorus, multiple vocal characters trading lines`。
- 双语歌词:主歌韩文叙事、副歌英文/中文 hook,或反过来——直接在歌词框混写。
- **要反高潮**(ballad):终副歌标签改 `[Final Chorus - stripped back to just vocal and piano, quiet, intimate ending]`。
