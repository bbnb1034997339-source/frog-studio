# 脚手架:朋克(pop punk / 直冲)

> 起点,不是模具。骨架留白,自由发挥。

## 风格框(起点,描述式写法)
```
An energetic but warm pop-punk track at about 150 BPM, performed by one three-piece band in a small gritty room. Live drums keep a steady pulse, bass locks to the kick, and distorted guitar leaves brief gaps around the vocal. A glockenspiel quietly previews the hook and returns between phrases. The same raspy mid-range male singer carries every section; a small gang response joins only on the chorus hook. Keep natural dynamics without rushing the ending. No EDM or glossy vocal processing.
```
> **点睛乐器**:默认钢片琴(glockenspiel,在失真里冒出来的反差感),可换颤音琴(vibraphone)/口琴(harmonica)。

## 动态走向
默认**忽收忽放型**(▃▅▃▇▁▇):主歌冲但不满,Bridge 收到人声+单件乐器,终副歌恢复完整乐队,保持原速度、声线与核心 riff。

## 留白骨架
```
[Intro - guitar feedback, then riff]

[Verse 1 - driving but not full volume]
〔短句直给,密一点;行内错落〕

[Pre-Chorus - building, drums double time]
〔起势〕

[Chorus - full band, punchy hook, restrained gang-vocal response]
〔一句能喊的 hook,平实有劲;+ gang vocals / 语气词 (hey!)〕

[Verse 2 - driving but not full volume]
〔与 V1 段间对仗〕

[Pre-Chorus]

[Chorus - full blast]

[Bridge - everything drops, just vocal and single guitar]
〔态度转折/留白,最安静处;可换打法——缩到极简〕

[Guitar Solo - short variation of the main hook, full distortion, same tempo]

[Final Chorus - same tempo and singer, full band returns, gang vocals answer the hook]
〔末段恢复完整乐队,用回应式群唱增加能量,不要整体加速或持续吼高音〕
(hey! hey! hey!)

[Outro - riff + feedback, abrupt stop]
```

## 调参
- 更冲:改成 `fast, aggressive punk with a shouted vocal`,副歌段落标签 `[Chorus - gang vocals, shout-along]`,怪异参考度提到65。
- 想带 grunge 味:降到 mid-tempo,在描述里加 `fuzzy guitar with a dynamic soft-loud structure`。
- **换点睛乐器**:glockenspiel 换成 `a lonely harmonica cutting through the noise` 或 `a shimmering vibraphone in the breakdown`。
