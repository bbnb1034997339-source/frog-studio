# 脚手架:R&B(neo-soul,慵懒)

> 起点,不是模具。骨架留白,自由发挥。

## 风格框(起点,描述式写法)
```
A mellow, hazy neo-soul track at about 75 BPM in one warm, dry studio room. Live drums hold a steady syncopated pocket while fretless bass sits slightly behind; felt piano leaves space for a vibraphone to answer only in vocal gaps. The same breathy mid-range male singer uses natural breath breaks and restrained phrase endings throughout. Intimate verses open gradually into a fuller chorus with light response harmonies, without changing key, tempo, register, or vocal timbre. No EDM or heavy autotune.
```
> 人声性别用界面按钮设;音色细节(气声/沙哑/慵懒)用描述句补。
> **点睛乐器**:默认颤音琴(vibraphone),可换竖琴(harp)/口风琴(melodica)。

## 动态走向
默认**渐进升华型**:主歌极亲密(钢琴+人声+vibraphone)→ 副歌加和声铺开 → 终副歌最丰满。

## 留白骨架
```
[Intro - felt piano + vibraphone, intimate]

[Verse 1 - sparse, just piano and vocal, close and dry]
〔具体画面,慵懒;行内错落;密集短句〕

[Pre-Chorus - bass and drums ease in]
〔起势,字数渐展〕

[Chorus - full groove, layered harmonies, vibraphone swells]
〔支点意象,平实别致;舒展拉长;+语气词 (mmm)〕

[Verse 2 - sparse again]
〔与 V1 段间对仗〕

[Pre-Chorus]

[Chorus]

[Bridge - everything drops to just vocal and vibraphone]
〔留白;可换打法——缩到极简或换成排比长句〕

[Vibraphone Solo]   （或 [Piano Solo] / [Sax Solo]）

[Final Chorus - lushest, most harmonies, vibraphone prominent]
〔末段+和声推高潮〕
(mmm... oh...)

[Outro - just vocal and vibraphone, fading]
```

## 调参
- 更慵懒:在描述里加 `smoky, late-night, sultry atmosphere`。
- 加律动:在描述里加 `syncopated neo-soul groove with finger snaps`。
- 中英混:Hook 可换英文短句,主歌中文叙事。
- **换点睛乐器**:vibraphone 换成 `delicate harp arpeggios` 或 `a soft melodica melody`。
