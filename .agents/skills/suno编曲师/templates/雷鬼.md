# 脚手架:雷鬼(roots reggae / dub)

> 起点,不是模具。reggae 的灵魂是 offbeat skank + deep dub bass + one-drop drums,慵懒而深邃。骨架留白,自由发挥。

## 风格框(起点,描述式写法)
```
A laid-back roots reggae track at about 75 BPM. One-drop drums hold a steady pocket, deep round bass sits slightly behind, and offbeat guitar leaves plenty of air. A soft melodica answers only in vocal gaps and reprises the main hook. The same relaxed mid-range male singer remains identifiable through restrained delay throws in one consistent dub space; effects appear at phrase endings rather than washing over every line. No EDM pulse or heavy autotune.
```
> 想要更深邃的 dub 味:描述改成纯器乐感,`A spacious dub track — heavy bass, echoing snare hits, swirling delay and reverb throws, sparse offbeat guitar chops. Instrumental and atmospheric, deep and hypnotic.`
> **点睛乐器**:默认口风琴(melodica,雷鬼经典),可换锡哨(tin whistle)/口琴(harmonica)。

## 动态走向
默认**一路淡然型**(▃▃▂▃▂▂▁▁):全程低位运行,靠 delay/reverb 空间感和留白取胜,不推高潮。

## 留白骨架
```
[Intro - skank guitar + dub bass, melodica melody]

[Verse 1 - sparse, rhythm guitar and bass]
〔短句,慵懒从容;行内错落;密集但放松〕

[Pre-Chorus - delay starts echoing, tension builds slightly]
〔起势,字数渐展〕

[Chorus - harmonies enter, melodica weaves in]
〔一个支点意象,平实有回味;舒展;+ 和声 / 语气词 (oh-oh)〕

[Verse 2 - sparse again]
〔与 V1 段间对仗〕

[Pre-Chorus]

[Chorus]

[Bridge - everything drops to just bass, delay, and melodica]
〔最留白,可只剩贝斯和 delay;换打法〕

[Dub Break]   （或 [Melodica Solo] / [Bass Solo]）
〔delay/reverb 扔回声,空间感拉满〕

[Final Chorus - subtle build, harmonies swell but never explodes]
〔末段微加和声,但不炸——保持克制〕
(oh-oh... yeah)

[Outro - bass line + delay throws, melodica fades into echo]
```

## 调参
- 更深 dub 味:在描述里加 `heavy echoing delays, reverb throws, sparse and atmospheric`。
- 更 roots:加 `acoustic guitar, harmonica backing, roots reggae warmth, 70s Jamaican feel`。
- 想混搭(如 reggae + rock):全局框只写公约数,桥段插 `[Bridge - laid-back reggae with offbeat skank guitar and dub bass]`(见 genre-mashup.md)。
- 漂向 pop:怪异参考度提到 55,多生成几版挑 reggae 味最正的。
- **换点睛乐器**:melodica 换成 `a tin whistle melody soaring in the distance` 或 `a mournful harmonica`。
