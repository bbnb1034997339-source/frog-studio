# 脚手架:嘻哈(lo-fi boom bap,可换 conscious/jazzy)

> 起点,不是模具。flow 要有稳定节拍、韵脚和轻微音高支点,不写无节拍的干声旁白。内容自由发挥。

## 风格框(起点,描述式写法)
```
A laid-back boom bap track at about 88 BPM, with steady syncopated drums, warm bass sitting slightly behind the beat, and sparse jazzy keys. A muted trumpet answers only in gaps between the sung rhythmic phrases and reprises the main hook in the outro. The same clear mid-range male voice stays melodic and breath-aware throughout, never turning into mechanical spoken delivery. Keep one late-night studio space with preserved dynamics. No trap/808 elements or heavy autotune.
```
> **点睛乐器**:默认弱音小号(muted trumpet),可换口琴(harmonica)/颤音琴(vibraphone)/萨克斯(sax)。

## 动态走向
默认**渐进升华型**:Verse 密 → Hook 放开;终 Hook 加和声/点睛乐器推上去。
也可**一路淡然型**:全程低保真氛围,不推高潮,靠质感取胜。

## 留白骨架
```
[Intro - beat + muted trumpet melody, 4 bars]

[Verse 1 - sparse beat, vocal forward]
〔密集短句 + 内韵,rap flow;行内错落〕

[Hook - melody opens up, trumpet weaves in]
〔记忆点,可旋律化;平实别致〕

[Verse 2 - beat slightly fuller]
〔与 V1 段间对仗〕

[Hook]

[Bridge - beat strips back, just keys and vocal]
〔转视角/留白;可换打法〕

[Trumpet Solo]   （或 [Sax Solo] / [Piano Solo]）

[Final Hook - fullest, harmonies join, trumpet soars]
〔末段+语气词推高潮〕
(yeah... 就是这样)

[Outro - beat fades, trumpet echoes out]
```

## 调参
- 漂向 trap:风格框末尾强调 `no trap, no 808 bass`,段落标签强调 `dusty boom bap drums`。
- 更 jazzy:在描述里加 `jazz-inflected hip-hop with upright bass and muted trumpet`。
- 叙事:在描述里加 `conscious rap with a storytelling feel`。
- **换点睛乐器**:trumpet 换成 `a mournful harmonica` 或 `a vibraphone adding a dreamy texture`。
