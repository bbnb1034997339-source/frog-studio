# 风格框写法(英文)+ 中英对照词库

风格框 = 声音世界。**英文填写**。**全部用描述性自然语言写,不罗列单词标签。**

## 唯一写法:描述式自然语言(V4.5/V5.5 都吃)

**写完整的句子/短语,把声音"长什么样"描述出来**,而不是堆逗号分隔的单词。

V4.5/V5.5 + 1000 字框对自然语言描述解析很好,尤其嗓音质感、乐队风格、制作细节这类需要"绕着说"的东西,描述式最准。单词罗列太干瘪,模型理解不了特征之间的关系;句子能把"什么乐器 + 什么质感 + 什么情绪 + 怎么演奏"串成有画面的声音描述。

> 红线:描述要写**"声学特征"**(口音/鼻音/咬字/音域/唱腔松紧/配器/年代/制作),**别写成叙事抒情**——否则触发歌词泄漏(被当歌词唱)。

## 描述维度(每句覆盖一个维度,自然串联)

写风格框时,按这些维度组织句子——**不是清单,是写作思路**:

1. **流派与年代**:这是什么类型的歌、哪个年代的质感。
2. **情绪与氛围**:整体情绪基调(可细化,见下)、空间场景感。
3. **配器与演奏**:用了什么乐器、怎么演奏(指弹/刷弦/失真/错落鼓点)。
4. **节奏与速度**:快慢、律动感、节奏型。
5. **人声**:嗓音质感(音色/唱腔/口音/音域/咬字)、性别用界面按钮设;**对唱/合唱/多声部**也在这里描述(见下)。
6. **乐团编制**:整支乐队/乐团的构成和演奏质感(见下),而非只罗列单件乐器。
7. **制作质感**:录音质感、混音定位、年代感。

**克制留白**:1-2 流派、2-3 配器、1-2 情绪足矣,多了模型会乱。不写满,留呼吸空间。维度 5/6 是**按需叠加**——不是每首都写对唱/大编制,有才写。

## 描述性写法 & 风格/嗓音模仿(重点)

想模仿某乐队/某人(如"Blur 的嗓音"):**不要写人名**(常被版权过滤或忽略),把"声音指纹"**翻译成声学特征,用完整句子描述**。

**翻译公式:口音/语种 + 音色 + 唱腔 + 音域 + 年代 + 乐队质感 + 制作感**

示例(Blur):
```
A 90s Britpop track with jangly distorted guitars, driving bass, and live drums. The male vocal is slightly nasal with a laid-back, cheeky cockney-tinged drawl — melodic and conversational. The overall tone is bittersweet but energetic and cool, with lo-fi analog warmth. Mid-tempo.
```

### 嗓音描述词库(挑着拼进句子)
- 音色:`nasal / breathy / raspy / husky / smoky / warm / bright / thin / full-bodied / gravelly`
- 唱腔:`laid-back drawl / cheeky / detached cool / emotive / restrained / crooning / shouted / whispery / conversational / slurred`
- 口音/语种:`British accent / cockney-tinged / American Southern drawl / Mandarin / mixed Mandarin-English`
- 音域/性别:`low baritone / mid-range male / high tenor / falsetto / soprano / mezzo`
- 处理:`clear pronunciation, tight phrasing / vintage tape vocal / doubled vocals / slight reverb`

### 乐队/制作质感词库
- 年代:`60s / 70s / 90s / early 2000s`
- 质感:`lo-fi, analog warmth, tape saturation, raw garage, polished studio, wall of sound, sparse and dry`
- 演奏:`jangly guitars, fuzzy distortion, fingerstyle, syncopated live drums, loose groove, tight pocket`

## 可选细节维度:氛围/场景 & 混音定位(开放,按感觉取用)

风格框不止"流派+配器+人声"。下面这些是**可叠加的细节调整维度**,用来把一首歌的**空间感、场景、混音质感**钉得更准。**这只是举例,不是固定清单**——按这首歌要的感觉自由延展。氛围/混音描述一般放风格框(全局)。

### 氛围 / 场景空间感(scene & ambience)
- 演唱会/体育场:`live stadium recording in one consistent arena space; the crowd joins only on the main hook while the lead vocal remains identifiable`
- 小酒馆/livehouse:`intimate small bar feel, smoky pub atmosphere, close-mic vocal in a dry warm room, late-night, low crowd murmur, unplugged`
- 一个人在树下/户外:`solitary outdoor feel with open air, a gentle breeze, faint birdsong, spacious natural reverb, warm afternoon, lo-fi field-recording`
- 卧室/demo:`bedroom recording, raw demo, intimate, close and dry`
- 教堂/空旷:`cathedral reverb, vast, ethereal, distant`
- 雨夜/深夜:`rainy night, melancholic, distant city hum, late-night solitude`
- 篝火/公路:`campfire warmth, around-the-fire / open-road, driving, windswept`

### 混音 / 制作定位(mix & production)
- 人声定位:`vocals up front / vocal-forward mix / intimate close vocal`(向前) ↔ `vocals tucked back, washed in reverb, blended into the mix`(靠后融入)
- 低频:`deep punchy low end / warm round bass` ↔ `light and airy`
- 声场:`wide stereo, spacious` ↔ `narrow, mono, lo-fi`
- 质感:`warm analog tape, vinyl warmth / clean hi-fi, crisp / raw and gritty`
- 鼓:`punchy live drums, loose` ↔(避免)`tight quantized dance beat`

### 组合示例
- 树下独唱 + 人声向前:`A solitary indie folk track with an open-air outdoor feel — gentle breeze, spacious natural reverb. Built on fingerstyle acoustic guitar with a warm, intimate male vocal sitting up front and close in the mix. Lo-fi warmth throughout, slow, organic.`
- 小酒馆现场:`A bluesy folk rock track with the intimate feel of a small bar — close-mic raspy male vocal in a dry, warm room with low crowd murmur. Brushed drums, unplugged, mid-tempo.`
- 体育场大合唱:`An anthemic stadium rock track in one consistent arena space. The crowd joins only on the main hook, while the same mid-range lead remains identifiable and the band holds a steady tempo.`

### 更多"名字→特征"翻译范例
- 朴树感:`A 90s Chinese indie folk track with a warm, slightly raspy male vocal delivered in a plain-spoken, understated way. Fingerstyle acoustic guitar, nostalgic tone.`
- Radiohead 感:`An atmospheric alt-rock track with a fragile, emotive falsetto male vocal. Layered guitars and electronic textures, melancholic and spacious.`
- 周杰伦 R&B 感:`A Chinese R&B track with a mumbled, slurred mid-range male vocal over a syncopated groove. Strings and piano, urban and smooth.`

## 扩展维度:情绪细化 & 氛围场景

下面是**更细的情绪颗粒度**和**更多氛围场景**,全部用描述性句子写,写进风格框时挑贴合的织进描述里。

### 情绪细化(emotional nuances)
不止"开心/难过",把情绪的**质感、温度、走势**描述出来:

- 孤独而温暖:`A warm but lonely mood — intimate solitude with a faint glow of comfort underneath.`
- 倔强/不甘:`A stubborn, defiant energy — clenched-fist emotion that refuses to give in, raw and urgent.`
- 释怀/放下:`A reflective, letting-go mood — bittersweet but ultimately at peace, like exhaling after holding breath.`
- 暧昧/心动:`A fluttery, unsure feeling — the nervous warmth of something just beginning, delicate and tentative.`
- 压抑/暗涌:`A brooding, restrained mood — emotion held tightly under the surface, tense and simmering.`
- 飒/洒脱:`A cool, unbothered swagger — confident and carefree, moving on without looking back.`
- 破碎感:`A fragile, cracking-at-the-edges emotion — beautiful but nearly broken, raw and exposed.`
- 治愈/暖:`A soothing, comforting warmth — gentle and safe, like being held on a hard day.`
- 荒诞/戏谑:`A wry, tongue-in-cheek tone — playful and slightly absurd, not taking itself seriously.`
- 史诗/壮烈:`A sweeping, heroic grandeur — vast and cinematic, with a sense of scale and destiny.`

### 氛围场景(更多,开放延展)
- 海边/海风:`Coastal atmosphere with a salty breeze, wide open horizon, waves in the distance, spacious and breezy.`
- 雪夜/寒冬:`A cold winter night with falling snow, hushed and still, breath visible in the air, isolated and pure.`
- 老唱片/复古:`Vintage phonograph warmth with crackling vinyl, sepia-toned and faded, nostalgic like an old photograph.`
- 城市/霓虹:`Urban neon-lit night, humid city air, distant traffic hum, electric and restless.`
- 校园/夏天:`Sun-drenched campus afternoon, cicadas buzzing, carefree and golden, youth in full bloom.`
- 告别/车站:`A lonely train platform at dusk, the hum of departure, bittersweet farewell in the air.`
- 梦境/迷幻:`A dreamlike, hallucinogenic haze — surreal and floating, time blurred at the edges.`
- 工业/冷峻:`A cold, mechanical, industrial atmosphere — metallic and stark, with a sense of weight and concrete.`

## 扩展维度:人声编排(vocal arrangement)

人声不止"一个男声/女声"。下面这些编排方式都写在风格框(全局描述里),要**段落内切换**则配合歌词框段前标签。

### 男女对唱(male-female duet)
两人交替或同时唱,描述清楚两人的音色和互动方式:

- 经典情歌对唱:`A male-female duet with alternating lead vocals — the male vocal is warm and mid-range, the female vocal is bright and clear. They trade lines in the verses and blend together in close harmony on the chorus.`
- 呼应式对唱:`A call-and-response duet between a raspy male vocal and a breathy female vocal — one asks, the other answers, building tension that resolves when they finally sing together.`
- 反差对唱:`A duet pairing a low, gravelly male baritone with a soaring female soprano — the contrast between rough and angelic drives the emotional push and pull.`
- 合唱式对唱:`A male-female duet where both voices carry equal weight, weaving in and out of unison and harmony — two distinct characters telling the same story from two sides.`

> 段落级分工(如主歌男、副歌女):全局描述写两人的音色,歌词框段前用 `[Verse - male vocal]` / `[Chorus - female vocal]` / `[Chorus - duet]` 切换。

### 多声部合唱 / 和声(multi-part harmony & choir)
不止背景和声,而是**多个声部交织**的厚实人声:

- 福音式合唱:`Gospel-style layered choir with rich four-part harmonies — full, soaring, soulful backing vocals that swell behind the lead.`
- 民谣合唱团:`A warm folk chorus of stacked vocal harmonies — three or four voices blending in a loose, organic, around-the-fire feel.`
- 大合唱推高潮:`A group response joins only on the main hook, slightly loose but coherent, supporting rather than replacing the identifiable lead vocal.`
- 细腻和声铺底:`Delicate two- and three-part harmonies woven beneath the lead vocal — subtle and atmospheric, adding depth without overpowering.`
- 阿卡贝拉式:`Multi-voice a cappella-style harmonies — voices layered to carry the whole arrangement, lush and intricate, no instruments needed.`
- 呢喃式叠加:`Whispered and hummed vocal layers stacked behind the lead — ghostly, intimate, like multiple voices in your ear.`

> 人声性别按钮只设主唱;合唱/和声的声部构成用描述句写进风格框,如 `backing harmonies sung by a mixed male-female choir`。

## 扩展维度:乐团编制(ensemble & band formation)

配器表给的是单件乐器;这里描述**整个乐队/乐团的编制和演奏质感**,适合需要"大编制"或特定乐队感的歌。

### 乐队编制(band setups)
- 三件套车库:`A raw three-piece garage band — guitar, bass, and drums only, playing live in a room together with a loose, energetic, unpolished feel.`
- 经典摇滚五件套:`A full rock band with two electric guitars, bass, live drums, and keyboards — warm and driving, 70s-style ensemble playing with a tight pocket.`
- 民谣乐队:`An indie folk band built around fingerstyle acoustic guitar, brushed drums, upright bass, with subtle accordion and violin accents — warm, organic, ensemble feel.`
- 嘻哈 live band:`A live hip-hop band — boom bap drums, warm bass, jazzy electric piano, and a horn section, all played live with a loose, human groove.`
- R&B/neo-soul band:`A neo-soul band with Rhodes piano, fretless bass, syncopated drums, and smooth horn stabs — warm, tight, late-night groove.`

### 弦乐 / 管乐编制(strings & horns)
- 弦乐四重奏:`A classical string quartet — two violins, viola, and cello — playing with intimate, expressive phrasing, warm and close-mic'd.`
- 大编制弦乐:`Lush sweeping string section with multiple violins, violas, and cellos — cinematic and grand, soaring over the arrangement.`
- 爵士管乐组:`A small jazz horn section — trumpet, tenor sax, and trombone — playing tight stabs and warm unison lines with a vintage feel.`
- 铜管推高潮:`A punchy brass section — trumpets and trombones — blasting in on the chorus, anthemic and bright, driving the energy up.`
- 国风民乐:`Chinese traditional ensemble with erhu, pipa, guzheng, and bamboo flute — warm, ancient, with a spacious and elegant feel.`

### 大编制 / 电影感(large-scale & cinematic)
- 电影配乐感:`A cinematic arrangement with full orchestra — sweeping strings, warm brass, subtle percussion, and a lone vocal floating over the top. Vast and emotional.`
- 音乐剧感:`A theatrical, Broadway-style arrangement — full band with piano, brass, and strings, dramatic dynamic shifts, vocals front and center with expressive delivery.`
- 史诗摇滚:`An arena-scale rock arrangement where guitars, strings, and choir enter in stages around a steady rhythm section and one identifiable mid-range lead vocal.`

> 大编制容易出 AI 味:写清各声部的进入顺序、主次和统一空间,一次只增加 1-2 层。不要只靠 `organic/human/slightly imperfect`,也不要让所有声部持续最大音量。

## 本项目默认的真实感 / 反舞曲写法

不要机械塞入 `organic, human, raw, slightly imperfect`。每首至少写清两个**可听见的行为**:

- 时间关系:`steady live drums with the bass sitting slightly behind the beat`
- 乐器互动:`guitar and signature instrument answer only in vocal gaps`
- 演唱生理:`comfortable mid register with natural breath breaks and simple phrase endings`
- 空间连续:`one warm, dry studio room with consistent vocal distance`
- 动态保留:`preserved dynamics without brick-wall limiting`

反单一鼓点可写 `syncopated live drums, laid-back pocket`;只有确实容易漂向舞曲时才排除 `four-on-the-floor` 或 `EDM kick`。否定项默认控制在 2-4 个。慵懒按需使用 `laid-back / mellow / hazy`,不要每首都自动 lo-fi。

> **防平淡 / 编曲张力**:风格框里还要描述**段落动态对比**(主歌收→副歌放)和**点睛乐器**(一件签名音色),让歌有起伏和记忆点。详见 `reference/arrangement-dynamics.md`。
>
> **先锁一致性再做动态**:固定同一主唱的音色、颗粒、口音、唱法和舒适音域,并固定 BPM、拍号、key center 与 groove。高潮优先靠配器密度、和声和力度,默认不升 Key、不加速、不切换声线。全套真人表演逻辑见 `reference/performance-realism.md`,一致性英文约束见 `reference/consistency.md`。

## 成品示例(各流派,描述式)

- **民谣(慵懒)**:`A laid-back indie folk song in one intimate dry room. Fingerstyle acoustic guitar leaves space around a warm mid-range male vocal; brushed drums stay steady while bass sits slightly behind the beat. Natural breath breaks, simple phrase endings, mid-tempo.`
- **朋克**:`An energetic but warm pop punk track. Distorted guitar and driving bass over live drums, with a raspy male vocal and gang vocals on the chorus. Raw and anti-polished, upbeat at about 150 BPM.`
- **grunge**:`A moody 90s grunge / alt-rock track with fuzzy guitar and steady live drums in one gritty room. The same raspy mid-range male singer stays restrained in quiet sections and adds intensity without changing timbre in the fuller chorus. Mid-tempo.`
- **嘻哈(lo-fi)**:`A laid-back lo-fi boom bap hip-hop track with jazzy keys, warm bass, and syncopated drums over vinyl crackle. Clear male flow, human feel, mid-tempo at about 88 BPM. No trap elements, no 808 bass, no autotuned vocals.`
- **R&B(neo-soul)**:`A mellow, hazy neo-soul track with felt piano, fretless bass, and syncopated drums. Breathy male vocal with layered harmonies, warm analog production. Slow to mid-tempo, about 75 BPM.`

## 要避免的风格(写进同一个风格框)

suno.cn **没有单独的排除标签框**,所有内容都写在同一个"音乐风格"框里。想避免某种风格,**在风格框描述里加否定句**,而不是单独罗列。

> 原则:**先正向把要的风格钉死,再补一句否定**。否定句要具体(说清楚不要什么质感),别只写一个词。

写法示例:
- 防 trap 漂移(嘻哈):`…no trap elements, no 808 bass, no autotuned vocals.`
- 防舞曲化:`…not a dance or EDM track, no four-on-the-floor beat.`
- 防干声旁白:`…no dry unpitched narration; use melodic rap or narrow-range talk-singing over a steady pulse when conversational delivery is needed.`
- 防过度修音:`…raw and natural vocals, no heavy autotune, no robotic processing.`

常用否定(按需织进风格框末尾):
不要复制固定排除串。按歌曲只保留 2-4 项最可能漂移的风险;需要口语感时不要排除全部 rap,而要正向指定 melodic rap / talk-singing。

## 高级设置滑杆(suno.cn 实测)

| 滑杆 | 含义 | 高 | 低 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **风格参考度** Style Influence(默认50) | 多大程度死守风格框/参考音频 | 严格贴风格框,但会压制段落标签的局部覆盖 | 更自由,段落标签更易生效 |
| **怪异参考度** Weirdness(默认50) | 实验性/打破常规程度 | 出人意料、可能跑偏 | 规整安全、可预测 |
| **音色角色** Persona | 复用固定嗓音 | 跨歌保持人声一致 | — |

按本项目口味的建议:
- **常规单一风格**:50 / 50 起步。
- **混搭/段落反差**:风格参考度 **~40-50**(太高会压制 `[Section - genre]` 段落覆盖,接缝更死),怪异参考度 **~55-65**(给反差/创意空间)。
- **要稳、副歌不发散**:怪异参考度 ≤50。
- **去 AI 味**:无直接滑杆。靠演唱生理、乐手互动、统一空间、编曲因果、歌词人物感和生成后筛选共同控制;`organic/live/human` 只能作为辅助词。
- 满意的嗓音存成 **音色角色(Persona)**,系列歌保持一致。

## 人声性别(suno.cn 有独立按钮)
界面有 **随机 / 男声 / 女声** 按钮,设整首人声性别用按钮即可,风格框里再用描述句补充音色质感(`breathy, raspy, warm`)。要**段落内切换**人声,仍靠歌词框段前标签(如 `[Chorus - female vocal]`)。
